# Reflection — Agency over Throughput (2026-06-07)

## Two eras, measured

| era | cadence | pieces | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| May 2026 ("firehose") | improv every 12 min, ~120/day | 1085 | majority rejected by gates; explicit feedback ran 6 drop / 3 keep / 1 keep-iterate; ~500 reject_* artifacts in scripts/ |
| June 2026 ("evolve") | 45-min hypothesis-gated ticks on staging | 14 | the run the user reads as *intentional*; keeps (dla DLA×Gormley figure, len Lenia×Haeckel) came from named-intent hybrids |

Volume did not compound into quality. The 12-min loop optimized for ticks,
not for choices — pieces read as the engine spinning, not a person deciding.

## What the scored data says (248 improv ticks)

- **artist mode: mean 7.00 (n=70)** — channeling a named artist sensibility is
  the most intentional framing and scores best.
- surprise mode: 6.75 (n=91) — one deliberate turn works.
- **chain mode: 6.55 (n=87)** — word-drift without stated intent is the weakest;
  it reproduces the old random mode after gen ≥ 4.

## What the keeps share

1. A **named hybrid** — real technique × real artist (DLA × Gormley, Lenia × Haeckel),
   never "extrapolate from priors generically".
2. A **stateable intent** — you can say in one line what the piece is doing
   (standing figure accreting from drift; radiolarian symmetry self-organizing).
3. **Recognizable form, restrained palette, contemplative motion** — all already
   codified in taste.json principles; the keeps actually honor them.
4. What the drops share: haze/texture without subject (physarum×Anadol,
   GrayScott×Eliasson "haze blob"), pattern-as-wallpaper (Chladni×Ikeda).

## Changes made with this reflection

- Cadence: hourly evolve tick (crontab), replacing both the paused 15-min line
  and the 12-min firehose design. One deliberate piece per hour.
- MODE_WEIGHTS re-tuned from the 248-tick data: chain 0.25→0.15, artist 0.20→0.30.
- New taste.json principle: STATE INTENT FIRST — every piece begins as a
  one-line hypothesis and is judged against it.
- New pieces continue to land on `staging` for batch user review; a sibling
  agent demotes weak `main` pieces into staging — this loop only adds.
