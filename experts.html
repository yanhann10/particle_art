experts panel — what would a famous contemporary artist do?

Five distilled-artist personas critique every newly-mutated piece in a single Claude call. Each persona reads the piece in their own register, gives praise / concern / one concrete improvement directive. The directives feed forward into the next mutation's prompt as soft guardrails. This page traces what they've said, ranks their influence on the gallery, and tracks whether the panel is getting better at catalyzing pieces the user keeps.
