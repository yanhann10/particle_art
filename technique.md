# Genuary Technique & Pattern Reference

Genuary is a yearly creative coding challenge where artists make generative art from daily prompts.
These prompts are technique/pattern seeds — use them to drive new particle art directions.

## High-signal techniques for particle/shader systems

Prompts that map directly to implementable particle-art strategies (extracted across all years):

**Dynamics & motion**
- Flocking (2024/19) — Boids: separation, alignment, cohesion
- Chaotic system (2024/8) — Lorenz, Rössler, or Duffing attractors
- Genetic evolution and mutation (2026/29) — evolutionary branching in particle lineage
- Infinite loop / perfect loop (2023/1) — closed-trajectory particle paths
- What does wind look like? (2025/18) — curl-noise or flow-field advection

**Form & geometry**
- Signed Distance Functions (2023/8, 2024/29) — SDF-constrained particle shells
- Droste effect (2024/3) — recursive self-similar zoom
- Subdivision (2025/12) — iterative mesh/space subdivision driving spawn points
- Impossible objects (2024/24) — M.C. Escher-style topology
- Fibonacci forever (2026/3) — Fibonacci spiral / phyllotaxis spawn patterns
- Wallpaper group (2026/17) — crystallographic symmetry tiling
- Polar coordinates (2026/10) — radial particle fields

**Texture & surface**
- Dithering (2022/2) — ordered or error-diffusion dithering on particle density
- Sand (2022/15) — grain simulation with settling physics
- Pixel sorting (2025/31) — sorting-based visual distortion
- Glitch Art (2023/3) — deliberate data corruption aesthetics
- Moiré (2023/23) — interference patterns between overlapping grids

**Structure & pattern**
- Tessellation (2023/12) — space-filling tile patterns
- Packing (2022/12) — circle/square packing with collision
- Recursive Grids (2026/26) — fractal grid subdivision
- Organic Geometry (2026/25) — biomorphic forms from geometric primitives
- Abstract vegetation / Plants (2022/23, 2023/9) — L-system or differential growth

**Color & light**
- Generative palette (2025/16) — procedural color harmony
- Black on black (2025/4) — near-monochrome with subtle luminance variation
- Lights on/off (2026/6) — high-contrast illumination switching
- Create an invisible object where only the shadows can be seen (2026/15) — shadow-only rendering

**Reference artists (named in prompts)**
- Vera Molnár (2024/5) — algorithmic line art, systematic variation
- Anni Albers (2024/11) — textile/weave grid structures
- Hilma Af Klint (2023/27) — biomorphic spiritual abstraction
- Yayoi Kusama (2023/25) — obsessive dot repetition, infinity rooms
- Sol LeWitt (2022/7) — instruction-based wall drawing, serial permutation
